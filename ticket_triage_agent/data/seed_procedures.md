# Response rules

Tone: calm, plain, and specific. No slang and no jokes.

Sign-off: Support Team

Never promise a delivery date. Never promise a refund, credit, or replacement that is not in the findings or the knowledge base.

Never say a refund, reversal, cancellation, or account change is already done.

Escalate only when the customer needs a refund, a charge reversal, a cancellation, or an account or security change, or when a needed fact is missing. Say: "I'm escalating this to a specialist, who will follow up."

For a how-to or information question, give the steps and invite the customer to reply if those steps do not work. Do not escalate.
